---
name: Loans & Daily Notes
description: Loan tracking (given/received, pending/paid) and calendar-based daily journal
type: feature
---
- Loans table: person_name, amount, type (GIVEN/RECEIVED), status (PENDING/PAID), loan_date, paid_date
- Two sections: "People Owe Me" (GIVEN) and "I Owe People" (RECEIVED)
- Mark as Paid button transitions status and records paid_date
- Daily Notes: calendar-based journal with add/edit/delete per day
- Both accessible from sidebar nav (Loans, Notes)
